Shipping to the EU? Prep for mandatory PIDs

Starting November 1, Product Identifiers (PIDs) will be required for all goods sold in a B2C shipment into the EU customs territory when the total shipment has an intrinsic value of EUR 150 or less. To avoid customs clearance issues, you must update your shipping processes now to ensure the required information is included for every line item.
