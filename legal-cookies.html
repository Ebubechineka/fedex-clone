Cookie policy

What this build stores on your device, and how to remove it.

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Applies to: this static demonstration build only.

1. Cookies

This build sets no cookies of its own. The pages are static and every interaction runs in the browser.

2. Local storage

Two features rely on localStorage rather than cookies: the shipment reference counter, so codes keep incrementing across visits, and the demonstration sign-in flag, so a refreshed page still shows you as signed in. Both are first-party, readable only by this site, and never sent over the network.

3. Third parties

No advertising, analytics or session-replay scripts are loaded. Images and stylesheets are served from this site\u2019s own host.

4. Removing stored data

Open your browser settings, find site data for this domain and clear it. The next visit starts from a clean state with the reference sequence back at one. Nothing else needs to be reset.

5. Why there is no consent banner

A consent banner exists to obtain permission before setting non-essential cookies. Since this build sets none, it shows a notice instead of asking for permission it does not need.
